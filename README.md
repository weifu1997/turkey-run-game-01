Gobble's Great Escape — a turkey escape game: one turkey, one farmer, and one Thanksgiving dinner to dodge. Run through the farmyard, the barn yard and the kitchen, jumping fences, pumpkins and glowing ovens, ducking falling carving knives, hiding behind hay bales when the farmer closes in, and grabbing corn on the way to freedom at 1500 m. Original canvas art, synthesized WebAudio sound, and a farmer who chases, corners and searches.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — Space or click to jump, ↓ to hide behind hay, ← → to pace yourself, M to mute, P to pause.
